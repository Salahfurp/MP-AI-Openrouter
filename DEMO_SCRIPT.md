# 3-Minute Demo Script

### 1) Open
**Say:** "This is a prototype AI assistant for applicants preparing a Master Plan Permit submission. It is grounded in the Urban Planning Permits Guideline, not generic internet knowledge."

### 2) Requirements
Click **Requirements**.

**Say:** "The assistant identifies the core submission package: Master Plan Report, GIS master plan, and project development budget in Excel, then points the user to the applicable guideline section."

### 3) Process
Click **Process**.

**Say:** "Instead of asking the applicant to read 139 pages, the assistant explains the customer journey: initial application, completeness review, Dubai 2040/public facilities review, alignment, final preparation and approval."

### 4) Interactive question
Ask:
`What happens if a modification exceeds 10% of the approved Master Plan?`

**Say:** "This is where the assistant starts becoming useful rather than being a simple chatbot. It can explain the threshold, identify potentially triggered studies and ask for project details to refine the guidance."

### 5) Close
**Say:** "The POC can evolve from Q&A into an AI Permit Assistant: project intake → permit selection → personalized checklist → document completeness check → guided submission."
