The competent authority and associated responsibilities
1.
The Dubai Municipality (DM) is the Emirate’s competent planning 
authority pursuant to Law 16 of 2023 and its amendments under Law 
No. 8 of 2026 regarding Urban Planning in the Dubai Emirate.
2.
DM’s Urban Planning and Quality of Life Department is responsible 
for establishing all the statutory policies and requirements that must 
be followed by all stakeholders in the field of urban planning. 
3.
Dubai Municipality will review the initial compliance with Dubai 2040 
Plan of all submissions.
4.
Dubai Municipality will review conformity with Public facilities 
provision standards across all jurisdiction for Master Plan Permit and 
Planning Permit
5.
DM has the right to request any additional requirements or applying 
any guidelines In coordination with planning authority subject to 
chapter No. (18) in Urban planning law.
6.
Each planning authority is responsible for approving the master plans 
for the lands under its jurisdiction
7.
Any deviations from the agreed master plan approach by the 
developer will be reported to the Dubai Municipality by the respective 
Planning authority.
8.
It is mandatory for all the Emirate’s developers to abide by the 
decisions of the Dubai Municipality or other planning authorities.

1.
Recommended studies for Major Master Plan Modification
20
1.
Parcel configuration
•
Where a developer requests merging or subdivision of land exceeding 10,000 sqm or 10% 
of the total project land area, a Master Plan Permit is required. All relevant studies and 
NOCs shall be prepared in according with the previously defined requirements.
2.
Gross floor area (GFA)
•
Changes to land use distribution without changing GFA (e.g., repurposing 20,000 sqm or 
10% of total GFA from commercial to residential).
•
Change to total GFA through increase (e.g., +40,000 sqm or <10% of approved GFA.
•
Reduction of 27,000 sqm or -10% of committed affordable housing GFA requirements 
All such cases require the studies as outlined in the previous table.
3.
Planning regulations and urban design
•
Requests to modify maximum building height (e.g., from 30 floors to 33 floors) where the 
change exceeds 10% will require a detailed review. This includes assessment of Urban 
Design, Public Facilities, an Environmental Impact Study.
4.
Transportation
•
Addition of a new plot access (e.g., introducing a third access point).
•
Modification of ROW , including increases of up to 25% of total ROW area.
•
Changing to Roads network layout, such as increasing road length by 25% of total 
network.
 All such cases require a traffic impact study and Environmental clearance, and other relevant 
technical assessments.
5.
Utilities
•
Where a Master Plan allocates utility parcels, any request by developer to relocate, 
add, or remove utilities plots, shall require a Utilities Impact Study and NOCs, in 
addition to obtaining from relevant authorities (DEWA , Etisalat , …etc.)
6.
Phasing
•
For a large-scale projects are planned in multiple phases, any proposed expansion of 
a phase exceeding 10% of the approved phase area shall require the applicable 
studies and approvals outlined above.
7.
Population Impact
•
Any increase exceeding +10% or 2,000 persons (residents ,workers and visitors) 
shall be considered a major modification to the master plan. Such changes require 
review and feedback on public facilities, along with traffic, environmental, utilities, 
and market impact assessments.

1.
What is the Villa Track (VT)?
1.
The Villa Track is a special route within the MPP process that allows developers to market villa units for by investors. Under this process, 
the remaining parcels within the same master plan are frozen and cannot be marketed or reserved until all the required approvals from the 
relevant authority are obtained.
2.
The Villa Track is based on Circular No. 2 of 2023, regarding The Mechanism for regulating procedures For Approving And alignment of New 
Development Projects In Dubai.
3.
The process is initiated only after the Developer signs a Tripartite Agreement with the Dubai Land Department (DLD) and the respective 
Planning Authority with the approval of the Dubai Municipality, RTA, and DEWA.
2.
VT application procedure and requirements
1.
Selection of the VT option in the MPP submission.
2.
Submission of an Authorization letter for handling dealing with villa 
parcels.
3.
Approval/sign-off of the plan by RTA signs plan for frozen parcels.
4.
Obtaining approvals from RTA , DEWA Water and Electricity and 
DM’s Drainage Department.
5.
Signing of the Tripartite Agreement between the Developer, DM 
Waste & Sewerage Agency CEO and Dubai Land Department.
3.
VT Customer journey
1.
The respective Planning Authority accepts the MPP 
requirements and regulations.
2.
A new application number automatically generated: Same MPP 
application number + VT.
3.
The VT SOS is sent to the relevant Authorities for approval.
4.
The customer attaches the signed 3 Tripartite party agreement. 
5.
Final approval is issued and sent to the customer.
2.
Villa Track applications (1)
(1) For General Rules and Regulations, please refer to Circular No. 2 of 2023.

Context for real estate development
82
When is this used?
A General Planning Permit authorizes the implementation of pre-
approved types of development works for:
a.
All land parcels, 
or 
a.
Selected land parcels 
in accordance with the requirements prescribed by Dubai Municipality 
(DM), and without the need to obtain a Master Plan Permit or a 
Planning Permit.
The General Planning Permit exempts low-risk master planning changes from the 
requirement to obtain a Master Plan Permit or Planning Permit. This includes:
1.
Land Use and GFA 
• Adjustments within approved parameters 
• Addition of affordable housing 
• Shifting between the same Level 3 (L3) land use categories 
• Adoption of approved land uses within designated incentive areas (e.g. Al Quoz) 
2.
Planning Regulations 
• Addition of easements within setbacks 
• Increase in setbacks without affecting GFA 
3.
Design Guidelines 
• Removal of boundary walls to enable pedestrian connectivity 
• Relocation of entry/egress points along the same parcel frontage 
The GPP is applicable only where the development is:
• Non-EIA classified 
• Outside special designation areas 
• Within the defined scope of the applicable GPP framework 
Note: The Planning Authority reserves the right to require an alternative permit 
application following review of the proposed changes.

6. Urban planning standards
125
Calculation Rules to test different Dimensions of the Master Plan (1/2)
1.
Project Area
a.
Project area = Total area of developable lands (Total area in site plan “for new projects” OR Total area of modified plots “for approved MP modifications” 
2.
Gross Floor Area (GFA)
a.
GFA = Parcel area X FAR (Note Once the GFA is fixed, FAR = GFA /  Parcel area) 
1.
Building Heights (Floors)
a.
Total floors (above ground) = Podium floors + Tower floors
b.
Development is acceptable if total floors comply with DCAA height limits 
4.
Building Envelope Test
a.
Envelope BUA = Parcel area X Maximum allowable footprint after setback X Total floors
b.
Condition: If (GFA × 1.25) < Envelope BUA → development is considered workable 
5.
Unit Calculation
a.
Units = (Land use GFA X Land use efficiency) / Average area of unit
b.
Land use efficiency = (Usable area of GFA / Total Area of GFA) %
6.
Population Density
a.
Density = Population /  Project Area

Calculation rules to test different dimensions of the master plan (2/2)
Suggested calculations approach. Please highlight if any alternative method is used.
7.
Average Population Calculation 
a.
From each Land use L3 units, Population = Number of units X Number of people per unit

Peak Population Calculation

9.

b.

c.
Total parking = Basement + Surface + Podium
i. Parking - Basement = (Parcel Area X Parcel efficiency after deduction for working space and foundation)/ 45 sqm X Number of basement floors
ii. Parking – Surface = Area allocated for surface parking / 35 sqm
iii. Parking - Podium = Podium floorplate/ 45 sqm X Number of podium floors

 Formats

ii. Example, for project title Marsa Dubai:
iii. Report (single file) MD–Draft–Master plan report–Rev1.0
iv. Drawings (separate) MD–Draft–Land use plan–Rev1.0

i. Uncompressed
ii. Compressed < 100mb
d. Submit one uncompressed file for the main drawings
i. Land use
ii. Phasing
iii. etc
e.
All tables should be provided in MS Excel format with the formula intact
Report Requirements
1.
Presentation
a.
Report should be in the same order of the checklist.
b.
Provide a Table of contents with page numbers.
c.
Use simple clear language to convey the analysis or key features of the image, 
map, or table that is supplied.
We do not require general description of the same. 
d.
All maps must be captioned, with legible annotations and the clear use of 
color showing:
i. Project area at the center of the frame
ii. Legend with all relevant items for the topic of the map marked including 
general requirements such as project/ site boundary, north point and qibla, 
and scale bar.

All tables much be captioned, with clear titles for each row and column.
2.
Clearly indicate any elements that should have been in the
