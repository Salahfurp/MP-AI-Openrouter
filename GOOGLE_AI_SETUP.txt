Master Plan AI Assistant - Google AI setup
===========================================

The V4 code supports two AI routes:
1) Google AI directly (recommended)
2) OpenRouter fallback

To use Google AI on Render, add these Environment Variables:

AI_PROVIDER=google
GEMINI_API_KEY=YOUR_GOOGLE_AI_STUDIO_KEY
GOOGLE_MODEL=gemini-3.8-flash

Recommended production model:
GOOGLE_MODEL=gemini-3.8-flash

Gemma 4 options available through the Gemini API:
GOOGLE_MODEL=gemma-4-31b-it
or
GOOGLE_MODEL=gemma-4-26b-a4b-it

If AI_PROVIDER is left as auto, the app uses Google AI when GEMINI_API_KEY or GOOGLE_API_KEY exists; otherwise it falls back to OpenRouter when OPENROUTER_API_KEY exists.

Your existing OpenRouter variables can remain in Render. They act as fallback when AI_PROVIDER=openrouter or when no Google key is configured.
